# Cost Allocation — Real Reporting That Makes the App Valuable

Owner-defined spec. This is what makes Ranch Manager Pro much more valuable than a basic livestock tracker: every expense is assignable to one or more dimensions, so reports answer real management-accounting questions.

## Expense assignment dimensions
Every expense should be assignable to one or more of:
- **Ranch or business entity**
- **Livestock species**
- **Individual animal or herd/lot**
- **Pasture**
- **Equipment asset**
- **Job or activity** (e.g. "Hay Production")
- **Date and vendor**
- **Expense category**

## Examples
- Diesel put in a tractor → assign to "Hay Production"
- A trailer repair → assign to that trailer
- Feed → assign to the cattle herd in a particular pasture
- Vaccines → assign to a livestock group
- Insurance premiums → allocate among trucks, trailers, tractors, equipment

## Reports it unlocks (meaningful questions)
- What did it cost to maintain the cattle herd this month?
- How much hay has each pasture or animal group used?
- Which truck or tractor has cost the most in repairs?
- Total cost per acre, per head, per bale, per mile, or per equipment hour?
- What did the ranch spend on fuel this year vs. last year?

## Relation to dashboard
Feeds the "Costs" section of the Daily Operations dashboard (current-month fuel, feed, vet, maintenance, insurance, total operating expense) and its cost figures per category.

## Core thesis
Allows the daily-use "what do I need to do today" flows to log costs at the point of action (barn, fuel tank, gate, shop), making the app faster and more useful than a notebook.
