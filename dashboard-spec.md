# Daily Operations Dashboard — "What do I need to do today?"

Owner-defined spec for the operational dashboard that anchors the product (faster than a notebook at the barn/gate).

## Dashboard sections

### Today's priorities
Overdue maintenance, vaccinations, pasture moves, low hay inventory, expiring registrations, insurance renewals.

### Livestock snapshot
Animal counts by species, births, purchases, sales, deaths, animals needing attention.

### Feed and hay
Bale count, estimated days remaining, recent usage, low-stock alert.

### Pasture status
Current livestock assignment, grazing days, rest days, observations, actions due.

### Equipment status
Equipment in service, maintenance due by hours/miles/date, open repairs.

### Costs
Current-month fuel, feed, veterinary, maintenance, insurance, and total operating expense.

### Calendar
Upcoming registrations, inspections, insurance premiums, vet events, planned work.

## Morning briefing (login experience)
When the operator logs on, the dashboard should read like a **morning briefing** — a short, scannable start-of-day briefing that answers "What do I need to do today?" at a glance. Lead with today's priorities and anything time-sensitive (overdue/expiring/action-due), then the snapshot sections, so the day can run without digging through forms. Fast, calm, and prioritized — not a blank form.

## Core thesis
Ranch owner keeps using the app only because it is **faster than a notebook or spreadsheet** while standing at the barn, fuel tank, pasture gate, or shop. Anchor = one daily question: "What do I need to do today?"
